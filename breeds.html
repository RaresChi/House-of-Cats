Cat Breeds

Ok, now that you have decided to buy a cat, you see some at the pet store, but you don't know which one fits you the best. Maybe you need a more energetic cat to play with or just a big fluffy sleeping buddy. Whatever it is, there is a cat breed that suits you the best. Check out some of the most popular cat breeds!

What is a cat breed?

Cat breeds refer to distinct varieties of domestic cats that have been selectively bred over generations to emphasize specific physical characteristics, temperament traits, or other unique attributes. These breeds typically have a defined set of standards recognized by cat breeding organizations, specifying characteristics such as coat color, pattern, length, body type, and facial features. Each breed often has its own history, lineage, and genetic profile that distinguish it from others. Cat breeds serve not only as a means for enthusiasts to appreciate and exhibit feline diversity but also as a basis for selecting cats with desired traits for companionship, breeding, or specialized purposes such as show competitions, therapy work, or specific roles like rodent control.

Here is a list of the most popular cat breeds:

1.Persian

Known for their long, luxurious coats and distinctive flat faces, Persians are gentle and affectionate companions. They have a calm demeanor and enjoy lounging indoors.

2.Maine Coon

The Maine Coon is one of the largest domesticated cat breeds, characterized by their tufted ears, bushy tails, and friendly, sociable nature. They are often referred to as "gentle giants."

3.Siamese

Siamese cats are known for their striking blue almond-shaped eyes, short coats, and distinctive color points on their ears, face, paws, and tail. They are vocal, intelligent, and affectionate.

4.British Shorthair

With their round faces, dense coats, and sturdy build, British Shorthairs are often described as teddy bear-like. They have a calm and easygoing temperament, making them great family pets.

5.Bengal

Bengals have a distinctive wild appearance with their spotted or marbled coat reminiscent of a leopard. They are energetic, playful, and highly intelligent, often enjoying interactive play.

6.Ragdoll

Ragdolls are known for their stunning blue eyes, semi-long fur, and relaxed, affectionate nature. They tend to go limp when picked up, hence the name "Ragdoll."

7.Scottish Fold

Scottish Folds are recognized by their unique folded ears, giving them an adorable and distinctive appearance. They are typically affectionate, gentle, and good with children and other pets.

8.Sphynx

The Sphynx is a hairless breed known for its wrinkled skin, large ears, and energetic personality. Despite their lack of fur, they are known to be affectionate, outgoing, and often seek warmth from their human companions.

9.Russian Blue

With a similar aspect with British Shorthair, Russian Blues have a striking blue-gray coat and bright green eyes. They are known for their gentle, reserved nature and tend to form strong bonds with their human family members.

10.Abyssinan

Abyssinians have a distinctive ticked coat with agouti hairs, giving them a wild appearance. They are active, playful, and highly intelligent cats that enjoy interactive play and mental stimulation.

Is there anything else?

Despite all the pretty faces you have seen in the previous images, some of the cats don't have a perfect and beautiful life, struggling each day to survive in the urban jungle. They are called Stray cats, and their cozy home are the streets, their warm little beds are dirty gutters. Some people do not make their life much easier, being abusive and mean to them. In 2022, the number of reports made to the charity about intentional harm to cats was 1726 - around five a day. This is up from 1387 in 2021, an increase of 25%. And we are only talking about the reported cases. The real number might be much bigger than it seems.

If you were thinking about adopting or buying a cat with a much cheaper price, consider visiting adoption centers or vets for stray baby cats, so they do not wind up on the streets just as their parents did. Some pet stores may offer them. The prices are not going higher than $20-$30, some of the, being absolutely free of charge
