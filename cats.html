Boring description

The cat (Felis catus) is a domestic species of small carnivorous mammal. It is the only domesticated species in the family Felidae and is commonly referred to as the domestic cat or house cat to distinguish it from the wild members of the family. Cats are commonly kept as house pets but can also be farm cats or feral cats; the feral cat ranges freely and avoids human contact. Domestic cats are valued by humans for companionship and their ability to kill vermin.

Interesting description

Research has shown that cats can provide emotional support, improve moods, and contribute to the overall morale of their owners. Cats are also credited with promoting socialization among older individuals and physically or mentally disabled people. Nearly 40 million households in the United States have pet cats.

History of Cats

The begginings

The written history of cats began with the beginnings of agriculture, about 10,000 years ago. Back then people had to store the grains they picked, which attracted mice and rats, which attracted cats. People, seeing that they could rely on them to eliminate parasites, began to become more friendly with them and let them in their households more

Gods in Ancient Egypt

During the time of the Romans, in Egypt, cats were literally deified. People decorated their cats with gold jewelry, built statues and sculptures for them, and in some periods, killing a cat could be punished with death. When their cat died, as a sign of mourning, the owners would shave their eyebrows and stay that way until they grew them back. There were also special cemeteries for cats, from where thousands of mummified cats were recovered, indicating how important cats were even to the Egyptian economy

Devils in the Middle Ages

During the Middle Ages, cats did not have the best reputation, being often associated with the devil and witchcraft. Thus, there were numerous rituals of cat sacrifice, such as: in Belgium there was a festival in which cats were thrown from the tower of the cathedral to ward off evil spirits; and in Paris, burning cats was a form of street entertainment with tens or hundreds of spectators
