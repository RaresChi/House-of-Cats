Lucky cat

Legend says that once upon a time, in Japan, a poor monk shared the little food he had with his cat. One day, a samurai lord, passing by the monk's temple, took shelter from the rain under a tree fixed at the entrance. Suddenly, he notices how the cat waves his hand, as if inviting him inside. It was probably just washing, but the samurai was intrigued by the cat's gesture and headed towards it. at that moment, the tree under which he was sheltering was struck by lightning. The samurai, relieved that the cat saved his life, became the founder of the temple, and today, the waving cat has become a worldwide symbol for good luck, being commemorated by various figurines

Spy cat

In the 60s, during the Cold War, the CIA launched a program through which they wanted to use cats as spies. The cats were implanted with microphones and radio transmitters in the hope that they would hang around the Russian embassy and hear important information. But the project, which cost 20 million dollars, was doomed to failure because the cats were too difficult to control

Six-toed cats

In the 30s, the writer Ernest Hemingway received a 6-toed cat as a gift from a friend, which he named Snow White. The author fell in love with the polydactyl cat and adopted others. Today, at his memorial house, there are dozens of cats descended from Snow White, most of them 6-toed.
